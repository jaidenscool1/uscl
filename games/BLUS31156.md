# Grand Theft Auto V (BLUS31156)

Disc 1.01 (APP_VER 01.00), update **1.27** installed in the app profile 2026-11-13 (not booted or profiled on it yet). Profiled 2026-11-06 on the uscl-rpcs3 rebase (upstream eee3f0f): first boot builds the
PPU and SPU caches (~3 min), then legal screens and Display Calibration, picture correct. The disc EBOOT decrypts
with `unself.py`; RPCS3's `--decrypt` silently skips it.

## How it goes online
Rockstar's own services (**ROS**, `rage` net code: cloud files, tunables, Social Club inbox/events), with PSN for
identity:
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| login proof | `sceNpManagerRequestTicket2`, service `UP1004-BLUS31156_00`, at boot (after the RPCN sign-in) |
| ROS | `prod.ros.rockstargames.com` TCP 443, HTTPS, looked up by `[RAGE] netThreadPool` right after the ticket |

`prod.ros.rockstargames.com` is still live (192.81.241.100, it serves GTA Online on current platforms). In the
first probe it wasn't in the IP swap list, so the game opened a TLS connection to it; keep it in the list.
Rockstar shut down GTA Online for PS3 in December 2021, so nothing on PS3 is expected to work against it.
