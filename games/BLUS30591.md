# Call of Duty: Black Ops (BLUS30591)

Disc 1.01, update **1.13** installed 2026-11-13 (not booted on it yet). Profiled 2026-11-07 (EBOOT strings).

## Online service
**Demonware** (Activision), the COD7 generation — same family as MW2 and BO2, so it reuses `uscl-dw`:
| What | Where |
|---|---|
| auth | `cod7-ps3-auth.demonware.net` |
| lobby / matchmaking | `cod7-ps3-lobby.prod.demonware.net` (also cert/dev) |
| NAT (STUN) | `cod7-stun.{us,eu,au,jp}.demonware.net`, `stun.{...}.demonware.net` |

Black Ops (2010, Treyarch T5) sits between MW2 (2009, "older lobby protocol", already working via uscl-dw 0013)
and BO2 (2012, title 18396, newer Demonware with auth3 HTTPS). Its auth/lobby is the bdLobby era like MW2, so the
MW2 path in uscl-dw is the closest starting point.

## Status (2026-11-07): reaches our Demonware, auth needs T5 handling
Booted on the rebased RPCS3 with the cod7 names pointed at `uscl-dw` (profile `~/.local/share/uscl/rpcs3-bo-probe`).
Black Ops boots to its menu and **resolves + connects to our Demonware** (`cod7-ps3-auth.demonware.net` → 127.0.0.1:3074,
binds 3075). But against the BO2-configured dw-server the **auth handshake doesn't complete** — it connects and closes
within ~30 ms, then retries every ~25 s. Black Ops is Treyarch **T5** (2010); the dw-server's PS3 auth handler
(`Ps3ForMmpRequest`) is **T6** (BO2). T5's auth/lobby differs.

So the Demonware transport and NAT groundwork are reused; what's left is **T5-specific handling** in uscl-dw.

## Update (2026-11-07): Demonware title id captured = 5185; title added
Booted on the bo-probe with the running dw-server (RUST_LOG=debug). Black Ops connects to `127.0.0.1:3074` and the
LSG (lobby service gateway) rejects it with **`The title id is unknown (value=5185)`**. So Black Ops's Demonware
**title id is 5185** — it sits in the Treyarch/IW PS3 range (MW2 PS3 `Iw4Ps3 = 5280`), confirming a `T5Ps3` title.
Added `T5Ps3 = 5185` to the bitdemon `Title` enum (uscl-dw **patch 0021**), which clears the unknown-title
rejection. The fact that Black Ops hits the **LSG** (not the older MW2 path) means it's the BO2-style bitdemon
lobby, so much of the BO2 (`T6Ps3`) work should carry over once the title is handled end to end.

**Live re-test is pending a dw-server restart** — the running dw-server is owned by another job and couldn't be
restarted here, so the title addition is built and committed but not yet re-verified against the game.
